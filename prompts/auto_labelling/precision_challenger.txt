Assign the primary intent and extract entities in the required JSON schema.
Decide the intent separately from the entities. A named food or exercise can
appear in a feeling log, general chat, or an out-of-scope utterance.

For each value, find the supporting words in this utterance. The example
utterances teach the format. Their goals, levels and equipment do not carry over.
Leave the slot empty when the wording does not support a value.

- Duration is time for one workout or exercise session. Include explicit
  seconds, minutes or hours. Exclude weekly frequency, programme length,
  race distance and work hours.
- Keep named exercise variants. Equipment must be stated as equipment, and
  body parts must be stated as anatomy or a target. Do not infer either from
  an exercise name. Gym is a location. Returning after a break does not give
  an experience level. Leave out explicitly excluded muscles or equipment.
- Keep the food phrase in its original word order, including its preparation
  or cut. Remove serving units and quantities. Do not use a nutrient name as
  a food or invent a specific food when the user asks for foods high in a nutrient.
- A request for macros means Protein, Total lipid (fat), and Carbohydrate, by
  difference. Add Energy only when the user explicitly asks about calories
  or energy. A generic request for nutrition facts leaves the nutrient slot
  empty. Use exact USDA names for requested nutrients. Examples are "Zinc, Zn",
  "Calcium, Ca", "Fiber, total dietary", "Sugars, Total", and "Cholesterol".

Check that the response has one valid intent and all eight entity lists.
Return only the JSON object.
